Hermes — Nataliya

A personal Google Workspace connection for a locally operated Hermes Agent.

What this integration does

This OAuth application lets its owner connect their own Google account to their personal Hermes Agent installation. It supports email triage and job alerts, calendar assistance, finding files, and working with Google Docs and Sheets when requested.

The application is operated by Nataliya, the owner of the GitHub account NNikolskaya. It is not an official application of Google or Nous Research, and is not offered for public signup.

Permissions and control

Depending on the permissions granted, the agent can read, send, and manage Gmail messages; read and manage calendar events and Drive files; read and edit Docs and Sheets; and read contacts. Authorization grants technical capabilities, not permission to perform every possible action. Actions remain subject to the owner's instructions and configured confirmation rules.

Google credentials are kept on the owner's machine, not on this website. Relevant information supplied to the agent may be sent to the configured AI provider or to destinations explicitly selected by the owner.

This website

This static website provides application information and privacy disclosures only. It does not accept Google login, collect OAuth callbacks, or store email, documents, or credentials.
